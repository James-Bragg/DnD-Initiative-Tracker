About the D&D Tool Assister

This tool helps Dungeon Masters and players manage initiative order during Dungeons & Dragons sessions. By tracking turns and making sure everyone acts in the correct sequence, it streamlines combat and reduces confusion at the table.

It is built to be flexible, so you can adapt it to your campaign. Stay focused on storytelling while the tracker handles the details of combat order.

How to use the tracker

  1. Add players. Enter each combatant's name and optional initiative bonus under Session Players.
  2. Enter rolls. Type each d20 roll into Initiative Rolls, or use Roll Blanks to roll for anyone without a value.
  3. Start combat. The tracker sorts everyone by total. Tied totals share a turn.
  4. Run the turns. Use Next Turn and Previous Turn to move through the order. The round counter goes up each time the order wraps around.

Your data

Players, bonuses, rolls and the current turn are saved in your browser, so a page refresh will not lose your place. Nothing is sent to a server.
